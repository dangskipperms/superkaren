The spec

What you give it

  • Any narration, any length.
  • Hints in brackets if you want them: [warm] Welcome back. [concerned] But something broke.
  • No hints: I read each sentence and pick the mood myself.
  • Or your own recorded voice instead of a generated one.

The voice

  • An adult narrator on ElevenLabs v3, or your cloned voice.
  • The same hints tell the voice how to say the line.

What you get

  • A 1080p video of him saying it.
  • Or a see-through overlay, to drop him in a corner of any video.

How he acts

  • About 25 face muscles, mixed. Never a stock pose.
  • Moods show at a third to two thirds of full strength: adult acting, not cartoon.
  • Each mood arrives a beat before the sentence, and relaxes after it.
  • Stressed words get a small nod or a brow lift.
  • Lips sync to the actual audio, sound by sound.

Between words

  • He blinks at a human rhythm: more when thinking, less when focused.
  • He breathes before sentences and glances away to think.
  • Nothing loops. Ten minutes in, he still never repeats himself.
  • He turns his head up to about a quarter turn.

Not in it

  • Hands, arms and body gestures.
  • Full profile turns.
  • A photoreal face. That is a separate route, on paid video models.

Done when

  • You watch a narrated render a few minutes long.
  • You call it watchable.

Your call

  • The look. This illustrated man, or a variant: beard, glasses, older, other hair. Each is cheap.
  • The voice. A stock ElevenLabs narrator, or a clone of yours.
